Add "Stand-up mode" with a "Body language notes" section. Read this whole message first, then do Parts A → E in order.

**What it is.** The teacher practises standing up, like in a real class, 1.5–2 m from the laptop. The laptop camera watches the teacher's upper body and face while they talk. The review then adds "ملاحظات لغة الجسد / Body language notes", each tied to the turn where it happened. The notes cover:
- back turned to the class;
- reading from notes;
- looking down or away while asking a question;
- hands in pockets or out of sight;
- arms crossed;
- hands that barely move, or never stop;
- touching the face.

## Ground rules

- **Off by default.**
  - Normal mode (hold-to-talk at the desk) must behave exactly as it does today.
  - Stage mode (?demo=1) never turns it on.
- **Body language notes are tips, never part of the score.** They never change the judge, the badges, the kids or the plan checklist.
- **Privacy.**
  - Video never leaves the device, and it is never recorded or stored. Only per-turn numbers are saved.
  - No Permissions-Policy header may block the camera.
- **Inclusion.**
  - Never shame. Notes are gentle, optional and easy to switch off.
  - Teachers move differently (disability, culture, style), so notes fire only on clear extremes.
- **Design system.**
  - Ink and ink-blue only for everything new; status colours stay on kid badges.
  - No emoji; lucide icons only.
  - Arabic RTL rules, Western digits, numbers inside `<bdi dir="ltr">`.
  - All copy goes in i18n (Arabic and English).
- **Database changes are additive only.**
- **Testing the camera:** it may not work inside Replit's Preview pane (an iframe). Test in a new tab and on the published URL.
- **After each part:**
  - run `pnpm test:judge -- --rows 1,2,3,4,7` and `pnpm test:plan`;
  - take screenshots (1280×720, Arabic and English);
  - show me.

## PART A — The library and the detector

1. **The library.**
   - Use Google's open-source MediaPipe Tasks Vision (`@mediapipe/tasks-vision`, Apache-2.0), pinned to the version you install.
   - Serve its wasm files and the model files (`pose_landmarker_full.task`, `face_landmarker.task`) from `/public/mediapipe`, so it works on the published URL without third-party CDNs.
   - These are downloaded open-source model files, not generated assets.
2. **`client/src/lib/body/detector.ts`.**
   - Open the camera once: `getUserMedia` video, 1280×720, `facingMode: 'user'`.
   - Run PoseLandmarker in VIDEO mode at about 12 fps, with the GPU delegate and a CPU fallback.
   - Drop to 6 fps if a frame takes more than 25 ms, and pause when the tab is hidden.
   - **Pose is the main signal,** because it works at 1.5–2 m.
   - **Head direction:**
     - when the face is at least 80 px wide, use FaceLandmarker to refine head direction;
     - otherwise, estimate it from the pose's nose, eyes and ears, relative to the calibration pose.
3. **`client/src/lib/body/features.ts`.**
   - Pure functions, run per frame. All distances are normalised by shoulder width.
   - Features: inFrame, waistVisible, faceVisible, headDown, lookingAway, backTurned, handsTogetherInFront, handsHidden, armsCrossed, wristSpeed, faceTouch.
   - **Starting rules:**

     | Feature | Starting rule |
     |---|---|
     | backTurned | Both shoulders visible, but the nose and both eyes have visibility < 0.5 and no face is found, for ≥ 1 s |
     | headDown | Head pitched ≥ 15° below the calibration pose, or the equivalent drop of the nose below the eye–ear line |
     | lookingAway | Head yaw ≥ 35° from calibration, or shoulder width < 60% of calibration (turned sideways) |
     | handsTogetherInFront | Wrists closer than 0.5 shoulder widths, between shoulders and hips, in front of the torso (holding paper or a phone) |
     | handsHidden | Hips visible, and both wrists either not visible (visibility < 0.4) or within 0.35 shoulder widths of their hip, for ≥ 5 s |
     | armsCrossed | Each wrist within 0.6 shoulder widths of the opposite elbow, between shoulders and hips, for ≥ 3 s |
     | gesturing | Either visible wrist, above the hips, moving faster than 0.6 shoulder widths per second (smoothed over 300 ms) |
     | faceTouch | A wrist within 0.5 shoulder widths of the nose, above the shoulders. Count each new touch (at least 1.5 s apart) |

   - Put every threshold in ONE file, `shared/bodyRules.ts`, so we can tune them.
4. **Per-turn metrics.**
   - Collect them ONLY while the teacher is talking (recording on).
   - For each spoken turn, save:
     - **talkMs**, and **seenPct** (the share of frames with the person in frame);
     - the % of talking time for headDown, lookingAway, backTurned, handsTogetherInFront, handsHidden, armsCrossed and gesturing;
     - **faceTouches**;
     - the same flags for the LAST 3 seconds of the turn (questions usually come at the end).
   - Typed turns have no body metrics.

## PART B — Setting up stand-up mode

1. **The switch on the Plan page:** "وضع الوقوف / Stand-up mode", with the line "تدريب وقوفاً مثل الصف الحقيقي، مع ملاحظات لغة الجسد / Practise standing, like a real class, with body language notes". Remember the choice in localStorage (inside try/catch).
2. **Turning it on opens "جهّز الكاميرا / Set up your camera".**
   - A mirrored camera preview with a faint waist-up body outline.
   - Live chips (an ink ✓, or grey): in frame, face visible, waist visible, lighting OK (the frame isn't too dark or backlit).
   - The checklist:
     - "الكاميرا بمستوى الصدر (اللابتوب على كتب أو رف) / Camera at chest height (laptop on books or a shelf)"
     - "ارجع لورا 1.5–2 متر لحتى يبين خصرك / Step back 1.5–2 m until your waist shows"
     - "الضو قدّامك، مش وراك / Light in front of you, not behind you"
     - "مايك قريب منك (سماعات أو مايك مشبوك) / A mic close to you (earbuds or a clip mic)"
     - "للحكي: ريموت عرض أو PageDown، أو الحكي بدون إيدين / To talk: a presentation clicker or PageDown, or hands-free"
   - **Calibration:**
     - "وقّف بشكل طبيعي وطلّع عالشاشة 3 ثواني / Stand naturally and look at the screen for 3 seconds";
     - then a 3-2-1 countdown;
     - the calibration pose is saved for this lesson.
   - **Two lines to show:**
     - "الكاميرا بتضل على جهازك. ما منسجّل ولا منبعث فيديو؛ منحفظ بس أرقام زي «وجهك للصف 70% من الوقت». / Your camera stays on this device. Nothing is recorded or sent; we only keep numbers like 'facing the class 70% of the time'."
     - "ملاحظات لغة الجسد نصائح اختيارية، وما بتدخل بالنتيجة. / Body language notes are optional tips and never part of your score."
   - **If the camera is blocked or missing,** show a friendly card and offer to continue without stand-up mode.
3. **Data:** lessons get a nullable boolean `standup`, and POST /api/lessons accepts it.

## PART C — Teaching in stand-up mode

1. **Hands-free talking.** This applies only in stand-up mode; normal hold-to-talk stays untouched.
   - **Default: toggle talk.** A presentation clicker, PageDown or Space: one press starts, the next stops. While recording, show a big, readable "الصف بسمعك… / The class is listening…" state.
   - **Option "الحكي بدون إيدين / Hands-free":**
     - start recording when the mic level stays above −40 dBFS for 150 ms;
     - stop after 1.4 s below −48 dBFS;
     - ignore anything under 1 s, and cap a turn at 45 s.
   - **Pause hands-free listening:**
     - while kids' audio plays, and for 400 ms after;
     - while waiting for the class.

     That way the kids' voices never trigger it.
2. **Big-text layout:** use the spec's stage font sizes, so the class is readable from 2 m.
3. **Self-view tile.**
   - In stand-up mode, a small mirrored self-view tile (about 200×112) takes the place of the "Type instead" button. Typing stays available with the T key.
   - The tile shows an ink dot with "مبين ✓ / In frame ✓", or a grey "مش مبين / Not in frame".
   - **Optional live nudges:** a switch on the tile, on by default and off in Calm mode.
     - One quiet ink text chip shows on the tile for 3 s, at most one per turn.
     - Examples: "ظهرك للصف / Your back is to the class", "عيونك عالورقة / Eyes on your notes", "إيديك بجيوبك / Hands in pockets".
   - Nothing else on screen changes.
4. **Saving per-turn metrics.**
   - After each turn's `done`, POST `/api/lessons/:id/body` with `{turnNo, metrics}`.
   - New table: `body_turns(lesson_id uuid fk cascade, turn_no int, metrics jsonb, unique(lesson_id, turn_no))`.
   - Redo deletes the redone turn's row.

## PART D — The review: "ملاحظات لغة الجسد / Body language notes"

1. **`shared/bodyNotes.ts`.**
   - ONE pure function turns the saved per-turn metrics, plus each turn's transcript and check type, into notes.
   - Only turns where the teacher was in frame for ≥ 60% of talking time count.
   - The thresholds come from `shared/bodyRules.ts`.

| Note | Fires when | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|---|
| asking | A turn with check type question or yes_no, where the last 3 s were headDown, lookingAway or backTurned | "When you asked «{first 8 words}…», you weren't looking at the class. Next time: look at them when you ask, then wait." | "لما سألت «{أول 8 كلمات}…» ما كان نظرك على الصف. المرة القادمة: النظر للصف أثناء السؤال، ثم الانتظار." |
| back | backTurned ≥ 20% of a turn's talking time | "Your back was to the class in turn {n}. Next time: face the class while you explain." | "كان ظهرك للصف في المداخلة {n}. المرة القادمة: الشرح والوجه للصف." |
| reading | headDown ≥ 50% of a turn AND handsTogetherInFront ≥ 40% | "You read from your notes for most of turn {n}. Next time: glance at them, look up, then talk to the class." | "قرأت من الورقة معظم المداخلة {n}. المرة القادمة: نظرة سريعة للورقة، ثم الكلام للصف." |
| looking down | headDown ≥ 50% of a turn (hands free) | "You looked down for most of turn {n}. Next time: look up at your class." | "كان نظرك للأسفل معظم المداخلة {n}. المرة القادمة: النظر للصف." |
| pockets | handsHidden ≥ 50% of all talking time | "Your hands were in your pockets or out of sight for most of the lesson. Next time: keep them free to show the idea." | "كانت يداك في جيوبك أو بعيدة عن النظر معظم الحصة. المرة القادمة: اليدان حرّتان لتوضيح الفكرة." |
| crossed | armsCrossed ≥ 30% of a turn | "Your arms were crossed in turn {n}. Open arms look more welcoming." | "كانت ذراعاك متشابكتين على صدرك في المداخلة {n}. الذراعان المفتوحتان أكثر ترحيباً." |
| still | gesturing < 5% of all talking time (at least 30 s of talking) | "Your hands barely moved. Next time: show the idea with them, like cutting the air into 2 and then 3 pieces." | "لم تتحرك يداك تقريباً. المرة القادمة: توضيح الفكرة باليدين، مثل تقسيم الهواء إلى قطعتين ثم ثلاث." |
| nonstop | gesturing ≥ 80% of all talking time | "Your hands moved almost the whole time. Next time: pause them on the key point." | "كانت يداك تتحركان طوال الوقت تقريباً. المرة القادمة: توقّف اليدين عند الفكرة الأهم." |
| face | 5 or more face touches | "You touched your face {k} times. It can pull attention away from the idea." | "لمست وجهك {k} مرات، وهذا قد يشتّت الانتباه عن الفكرة." |

   - **At most 3 notes,** in this priority order: asking, back, reading, looking down, pockets, crossed, still, nonstop, face.
   - **One strength, when true.** For example: "You faced the class while asking in turn {n}." / "كان وجهك للصف وأنت تسأل في المداخلة {n}."
   - **If seenPct is under 60% for most turns,** show this instead: "We couldn't see you for most of the lesson, so there are no body language notes. Next time, step back until your waist is in the frame." / "ما قدرنا نشوفك معظم الحصة، فما في ملاحظات لغة جسد. المرة القادمة: الرجوع لورا لحتى يبين الخصر بالكاميرا."
   - **The "still" example** (cutting the air into pieces) fits fractions. For other topics, use a neutral one: "show the size or the steps with your hands".
2. **Review page.** Add a card "ملاحظات لغة الجسد / Body language notes" right after "Your plan vs. your lesson", only for stand-up lessons.
   - **4 small stats,** in ink, with numbers in `<bdi>`:
     - "مواجهة الصف / Facing the class {x}%"
     - "النظر للأسفل / Looking down {x}%"
     - "اليدان ظاهرتان / Hands in view {x}%"
     - "حركة اليدين / Hand movement {x}%"
   - **Then** the strength line, then up to 3 notes. Each note has a turn chip; tapping it shows the teacher's words in that turn.
   - **Footnote (14px):** "قيست على جهازك من نقاط الجسم، بدون تسجيل فيديو، وما بتدخل بالنتيجة. مستوحاة من أبحاث إيماءات المعلّم ولغة الجسد في التدريس المصغّر. / Measured on your device from body landmarks, with no video recorded, and not part of your score. Informed by research on teacher gestures and nonverbal cues in microteaching."
   - **Hide this card in the shared view.** It's personal.
